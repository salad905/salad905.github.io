Help — Vase Dog Labeler

What counts as a dog?

Label an image Dog only if it clearly shows a domestic dog (canis lupus familiaris). The following animals are NOT dogs and should be labeled "No dog" or "Not sure":

  • Lions, panthers, leopards
  • Wolves, foxes, jackals
  • Horses, donkeys
  • Boars, deer, goats
  • Any other wild animal

"Not sure" is always a correct answer — never guess. If you cannot tell whether the animal is a domestic dog or something else, choose Not sure.
